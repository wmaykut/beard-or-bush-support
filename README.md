# Beard or Bush? — Support

Welcome to the official support page for the *Beard or Bush?* app!

This is the place for answers to your most pressing (or most ridiculous) questions about the game that’s 50% foliage, 50% facial hair, and 100% confusing.

---

## 🧐 What is this app?

*Beard or Bush?* is a swipe-based quiz game where you're shown a zoomed-in photo and must decide — is it a **beard** or a **bush**?  
Swipe left or right to guess. Laugh. Cry. Swipe again.

---

## ❓ Frequently Asked Questions

### Q: How do I play?
A: Swipe left for **Beard**, swipe right for **Bush**. That's it. Seriously.

### Q: Is this a serious quiz?
A: No. It's absurd. That’s the point.

### Q: Will I learn anything?
A: Only about yourself.

### Q: I think one of the answers is wrong.
A: Then you’ve truly understood the spirit of the game.

### Q: Does this game collect any personal data?
A: Nope. No logins, no tracking, no nonsense. Just swiping.

### Q: Can I submit my own photo?
A: Not in this version, but we love the way you think.
